ABOUT THE PROJECT

Data-driven exploration of distant worlds

Cosmos Atlas is an interactive portfolio project created to explore confirmed exoplanets through real astronomical data, visualization and exploratory analytics.

🌌

The Project

Cosmos Atlas transforms exoplanet data into an interactive experience where users can explore planets, compare distant worlds and analyze patterns in discoveries and planetary systems.

🔭

The Data

The project uses confirmed exoplanet information obtained from the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Planetary and stellar parameters are used to generate indicators, charts, rankings and interactive comparisons.

📊

Data Exploration

The project includes discovery method analysis, historical discovery trends, planetary system rankings and interactive filtering of confirmed exoplanets.

🧠

Educational Modeling

Cosmos Atlas also includes an experimental Habitability Potential Score created exclusively for educational data analysis.

The score does not determine whether a planet is habitable or capable of supporting life.
